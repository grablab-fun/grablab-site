About Grablab
Grablab is a real claw machine played live from the browser.Projects rent it for launch nights and load their airdrop into the capsules, and their holders grab the prizes live on stream.Before each play the grip strength is locked on-chain.Every win is checked by two sensors, a light beam and an NFC tag reader.The machine signs the result with its own key.The prize lands in the player's wallet in seconds, or the play is refunded.Built on machine.fun, with peaq and CodecFlow.
