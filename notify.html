Set it up on your device

iPhone and iPad

Apple asks for one extra step: the app goes on your Home Screen first. You need iOS 16.4 or newer.

  1. Open grablab.fun in Safari
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add
  3. Open Grablab from your Home Screen
  4. Tap the bell, then Turn on notifications, then Allow

Android

Works best in Chrome, and takes about ten seconds.

  1. Open grablab.fun in Chrome
  2. Tap the bell at the top
  3. Tap Turn on notifications, then Allow
  4. Tap Add Grablab to this device for the home screen icon

Windows

In Chrome or Edge. Firefox can show notifications, but it can not install the app.

  1. Open grablab.fun and click the bell
  2. Click Turn on notifications, then Allow
  3. Click Add Grablab to this device, or the install icon in the address bar
  4. Pin it to the taskbar if you like

Nothing showing up? Check that Do Not Disturb is off, and that your browser is allowed under Settings, System, Notifications.

Mac

In Chrome, Edge or Safari.

  1. Open grablab.fun and click the bell
  2. Click Turn on notifications, then Allow
  3. In Chrome, click Add Grablab to this device
  4. In Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock

Nothing showing up? Open System Settings, Notifications, and allow your browser.
