01What we mean by cookies

Cookies and similar technologies (local storage, session storage) are small pieces of data a website keeps in your browser. EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR) allow sites to store what is strictly necessary, and require your consent for anything else.

grablab.fun itself sets no cookies. It uses your browser's storage for two essential items only. Third-party embeds can store their own data, so they wait for your consent.

03Third parties without cookies

Some features make your browser contact other services, which then see your IP address but set no cookies through our site: jsDelivr (token logos), Jupiter (token prices on the Rent page) and your wallet extension when you connect. Details are in the Privacy policy.

04Change your choice

You can accept or refuse embedded content at any time. Refusing is as easy as accepting, and the site works fully either way, except that the physics lab will not load.

You can also delete stored data from your browser settings. If you do, we will simply ask again.
