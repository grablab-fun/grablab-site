When paid plays open

A play will create a public receipt on a blockchain that links a wallet address to the play and its result. Blockchain records are public and permanent by design, which means they cannot be changed or deleted later, including by us. We will update this policy before launch.

03Who else sees data

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the site and processes technical logs on our behalf.
  • Namecheap hosts our email (Private Email), so it processes the emails you send us.
  • jsDelivr serves some token logos and Jupiter (lite-api.jup.ag) serves live token prices on the Rent page. If you open the swap, the Jupiter widget (plugin.jup.ag) or, for other chains, the deBridge widget (app.debridge.com) loads in your browser and talks to them and your wallet directly. Your browser contacts them directly, so they receive your IP address.
  • SimArena (app.simarena.ai, by CodecFlow) runs the physics lab. It loads only if you allow embedded content, and then it receives your IP address and may store data in your browser.
  • Privy (privy.io, Privy Inc.) runs wallet connection and email sign in for us as our processor. It loads only when you open the wallet window or have connected before.
  • WalletConnect (Reown) relays the connection if you pick WalletConnect to link a phone wallet.
  • Your wallet provider (for example Phantom, Solflare or Backpack) handles the connection under its own policy.
  • Links to X, machine.fun and peaq take you to sites with their own policies.

Our fonts are served from our own domain, so loading the site does not contact font providers. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

04Transfers outside the EU

Some providers above are based in, or use servers in, the United States and other countries. Where that happens we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

05Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it, or delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including use based on legitimate interest;
  • send it to you or another provider in a usable format;
  • withdraw a consent at any time (for cookies, use ).

Write to privacy@grablab.fun. We answer within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In Romania that is the ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

06Children

The Service is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has sent us data, tell us and we will delete it.

07Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, keeps no user accounts and stores no wallet data on our servers. We never ask for seed phrases, private keys or signing approvals. Report a security issue to legal@grablab.fun.

08Changes

We will update this policy when the Service changes, in particular before paid plays open. The date at the top shows the latest version.
