06

House rules

Short list. If you are not sure, send it over and we will answer the same day.

Please do

  • Leave clear space around the mark, at least half its width.
  • Use the chrome mark on dark backgrounds whenever you can.
  • Write the name as one word: Grablab.
  • Use our machine renders when you show the product.

Please don't

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or outline the mark.
  • Put the chrome mark on busy photos or bright colours.
  • Use a generic arcade claw machine to stand in for ours.
  • Suggest a partnership or endorsement we have not announced.
